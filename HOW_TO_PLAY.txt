BCI BUSTERS - KEYS

IN THE GAME
  A or Left arrow ........ Kris walks left
  D or Right arrow ....... Kris walks right
  W, Up arrow or Space ... Kris jumps
  S ...................... Kris falls down faster (when he is in the air)
  F ...................... Starts the superpower. Stop at an obstacle first
                           (water, tree or box). The screen goes dark and a
                           flashing circle (the pad) appears.
  T ...................... Uses the power. The right friend comes to help:
                           Noelle for water, Ralsei for trees, Susie for boxes.
                           (With the headset this happens by itself when you
                           look at the cross in the middle of the flashing circle.)
  P ...................... Pause the game (press P again to go on)
  R ...................... Play again (on the end screen)
  M ...................... Go to the main menu (on the pause or end screen)


O = SKIP TO THE NEXT SCREEN (for demos)
  Press O on a screen to jump to the next one:
    1. Headset check screen ... goes to the training screen
    2. Training screen ........ goes to the loading screen (no training is done)
    3. Loading screen ......... goes to the game
  A returning player: the warm-up screen goes to the game.
  If you skipped the training, the brain signal is not used: press T for the power.


PYTHON FILES
